title Bull put spread: placing it, the stop, and the three endings
subtitle Example - SPY at 605, sell 600P / buy 595P, 30 DTE, 1.50 credit
dir down
group place Placing the trade
group out Possible endings

acct Account @wallet ~ Needs about 350 per spread in buying-power reserve (width 5.00 minus 1.50 credit, x100)
sell Sell 600 put (rounded) in place !accent ~ Short leg collects premium of about 2.80
buy Buy 595 put (rounded) in place ~ Long leg caps risk; costs about 1.30
credit Net credit 1.50 (note) in place ~ 150 per spread received up front. This is also your max profit
stop Stop order (rounded) in place !accent ~ Example rule - exit if spread value reaches 3.00 (2x the credit). Loss is about 150
watch Wait and watch (person) in place ~ Time decay works in your favor while SPY stays above 600

hit Stop hit (rounded) in out !accent ~ Buy back spread at 3.00, pay 300 vs 150 collected. Net loss about 150
otm Expires OTM (rounded) in out ~ SPY above 600 at expiry. Both puts expire worthless. Keep the full 150
itm Expires ITM (rounded) in out !muted ~ SPY below 595 at expiry without a stop. Short put assigned, long put exercised. Max loss 350

acct -> sell Sell to open
acct -> buy Buy to open
sell -> credit
buy -> credit
credit -> stop Place GTC stop
stop -> watch
watch --> hit SPY drops, stop triggers !accent
watch --> otm SPY holds above 600
watch --> itm SPY gaps through, no stop fill !muted

step Pick the vertical: Bull put spread means bullish to neutral. Same expiry, same type, two strikes 5 wide. | acct
step Sell the higher strike: Sell the 600 put. This leg brings in the premium and carries the risk. | acct sell acct->sell
step Buy the lower strike: Buy the 595 put as insurance. It is cheaper, and it caps the loss at the width. | buy acct->buy
step Net credit: Premium collected minus premium paid is 1.50. That is 150 in your pocket and your best case. | credit sell->credit buy->credit
step Add the stop: Brokers cannot attach a native stop to a spread order, so you place a conditional order that buys the spread back at 3.00. Risk becomes about 150, not 350. | stop credit->stop
step Wait: Each day theta shrinks the spread value toward zero if SPY stays put. | watch stop->watch
step Ending A, stop hit: SPY falls, spread value hits 3.00, the stop closes both legs. Loss of about 150. | hit watch->hit
step Ending B, expires OTM: SPY closes above 600. Both puts expire worthless and you keep all 150. | otm watch->otm
step Ending C, expires ITM: SPY closes below 595 with no stop. Max loss 350 because the long put offsets the short. Close before expiry to avoid assignment. | itm watch->itm
